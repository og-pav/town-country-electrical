7 Signs Your Home Needs a Switchboard Upgrade

Your switchboard is the heart of your home's electrical system — and in a lot of Northern Rivers homes, it's the oldest thing in the house still doing a full-time job. Here's how to know when it's time.

1. It still has ceramic fuses

Rewirable ceramic fuses were state of the art in the 1960s. They offer no protection against electric shock — only against gross overloads — and they have no place in a modern home.

2. No safety switches (RCDs)

Safety switches cut power in milliseconds when electricity leaks to earth — the thing that saves lives. Every circuit in your home should have one. Many older boards protect one or two circuits at best.

3. Breakers trip when you use two appliances

Kettle plus heater shouldn't equal darkness. Regular tripping means circuits are overloaded or the board can't handle modern demand.

4. Flickering or dimming lights

Lights that dip when the aircon starts can indicate loose connections or an undersized supply — both worth investigating promptly.

5. Buzzing, heat or burning smells

A switchboard should be silent and cool. Any buzzing, warmth or odour is a call-us-today situation — or after hours, a 24/7 emergency call.

6. You're adding big new loads

Induction cooking, ducted aircon, a pool pump, an EV charger — modern additions often exceed what an old board can safely supply.

7. Your home is 25+ years old and the board's never been touched

Even without symptoms, an inspection is cheap insurance.

Not sure where your board stands? Book an assessment — you'll get an honest verdict, not a scare campaign.
