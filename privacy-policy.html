Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-27

Town & Country Electrical Co. ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website and in the course of providing electrical services, in line with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

When you contact us we may collect your name, phone number, email address, property address and details of the work you're enquiring about. Our website also collects limited analytics data (pages visited, device type) if you consent to analytics cookies.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotes
  • To schedule, perform and invoice electrical work
  • To meet our legal obligations (including compliance certification records)
  • To improve this website (analytics, with your consent)

Forms and third parties

Enquiry forms on this site may be processed by our customer-management platform, which stores your details securely so we can respond to you. We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

Cookies

Essential cookies keep the site working. Analytics cookies (Google Analytics) run only if you accept them in the cookie banner, and you can withdraw consent by clearing your browser data.

Access and contact

You can request access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you at any time — email jordan@towncountryelectrical.com.au or call 0405 305 671.
